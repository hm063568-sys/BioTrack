VISION AI • EDUCATIONAL SCREENING

BioScan AI — visual learning assistant

Upload a clean, staged training image to identify visible objects and explore possible healthcare-waste categories for learning. The image model cannot determine contamination, contents, infection risk or a final waste stream.

IMPORTANT: Use only clean, staged training objects or synthetic educational images. Never approach, touch, open, sort, collect or photograph real healthcare waste for this project. Do not upload people, patient labels, medical records, blood/body-fluid images, real sharps or actual waste containers. The model's output is educational and must never be used as a disposal decision.
